# User Testing Feedback Session

**Goal:** Gather qualitative feedback from a real target user to identify UX friction, accessibility issues, and feature value.

---

## 1. Setup & First Impressions
*Task: Open the app, register a new account, and set up your basic profile.*

**What to observe:**
- Did they understand what the app does immediately?
- Was the registration flow intuitive?
- Were profile fields (allergies, emergency contact) clear?

**Notes:**
>

---

## 2. Core Workflow: Logging Data
*Task: Log a blood pressure reading, a glucose reading, and your current weight.*

**What to observe:**
- Was the "Add Log" button easy to find?
- Did they struggle with input forms (date pickers, number formats)?
- Did validation (e.g., blocking a 900 systolic value) make sense?

**Notes:**
>

---

## 3. Reviewing Data & Trends
*Task: Look at the dashboard, view past logs, and understand your current health trend.*

**What to observe:**
- Did they notice the "Health Alerts" card? Was it understandable?
- Were charts/tables easy to read for a non-developer?
- Did they try to edit or delete a past log?

**Notes:**
>

---

## 4. The AI Assistant
*Task: Chat with the AI assistant. Ask: "Does my blood pressure look okay this week?"*

**What to observe:**
- Was the AI's response helpful and accurate?
- Did the user trust the response?
- Did the AI pull in historical log entries (RAG working)?

>

## 5. Caregiver Flow (if applicable)
*Task: Register a caregiver account, send a patient invite, approve it from the patient account, then view the patient dashboard.*

**What to observe:**
- Was the invite/consent flow clear?
- Could the caregiver navigate patient data easily?
- Was it obvious how to switch between patients?
